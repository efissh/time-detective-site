Mona Radio

Your radio. Everywhere in Apple.

Mona Radio brings your Icom IC-705 to iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV over your local network: live spectrum and waterfall, receive audio, the S-meter, and frequency, mode and filter at a glance.

  • Supports the Icom IC-705
  • Connects directly over your local network
  • No account, no advertising, no tracking
  • Does not initiate radio transmission
