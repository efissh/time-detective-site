The loop

Guess it. Do it. Learn your time.

Time Detective keeps the lesson close to real life, so progress feels practical rather than performative.

01 / GUESS

Make a prediction.

Start with a child’s own sense of how long something might take.

02 / DO

Try the activity.

Move through a challenge or routine with a clear beginning and end.

03 / LEARN

Spot the pattern.

Use the difference between a guess and reality as a friendly clue for next time.

Privacy-first by default

More independence. Less data collection.

Time Detective does not require an account or cloud account. Child activity is stored locally on the device. The app has no ads, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.
