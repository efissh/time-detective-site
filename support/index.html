Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Family Unlock?

Open Grown-Ups → Family Unlock → Restore Purchases. Time Detective uses Apple’s purchase system to check for the one-time Family Unlock purchase.

Is Family Unlock a subscription?

No. Family Unlock is a one-time non-consumable purchase. It is not a subscription.

Does Time Detective require an account?

No. Time Detective does not require an account or a cloud account.

Where is my child’s data stored?

Time Detective stores child activity data locally on the device. Mona Apps does not operate a developer backend that stores this activity data.

How do I contact support?

Email monaapps.support@gmail.com. A grown-up can include the device model, iOS version, app version, and a brief description of the issue.
