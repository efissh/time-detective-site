Frequently asked questions

Why does DeskLens Learn ask for microphone access?

Microphone access is used when you start Live Listening and when you record yourself for Sentence Shadowing or another recording-related learning flow. The app does not silently record in the background. You can decline microphone access and still import audio or video.

Why is my Speech recognition language unavailable?

Speech recognition language availability is controlled by iOS and the device. Check that the selected source language matches the material, that your device supports it, and that the required Apple Speech resources are installed. The first use of a language may need an internet connection for iOS to download a resource.

Why does Translation need a language or model download?

Apple Translation resources may need to be downloaded the first time you use a language pair. Connect to the internet, keep the app open while iOS prepares the resource, then retry the translation. Apple manages this download; it is separate from a DeskLens Learn backend.

Why did my audio or video fail to import?

Choose a local file that iOS can open, such as a common m4a, wav, mp3, mov, mp4, or m4v file. Check that the file is available offline, is not protected by a format the system cannot decode, and that the device has enough storage. DeskLens Learn copies a successful import into its own local storage.

Why can’t I hear playback?

Check the device volume and current audio route, then try the exact word or sentence again. Confirm that the imported media contains an audible speech segment. If playback still fails, close and reopen the learning session and contact support with the file type and the step that failed.

How do I change the iOS app interface language?

Open Settings → Apps → DeskLens Learn → Language if your iOS version shows an app-specific language setting. Otherwise, open Settings → General → Language & Region and adjust your preferred languages. This changes the interface, not the learning/source or translation target language.

How do I restart or retry Apple Speech or Translation resources?

First confirm the selected language and your network connection, then close and reopen the learning flow and retry. If iOS is still preparing a language resource, wait for that process to finish; restarting the device can also refresh a stalled system resource. Contact support if the same supported language continues to fail.

Which iOS version does DeskLens Learn require?

DeskLens Learn V1 requires iOS 26.0 or later because its Speech and Translation workflows depend on system technologies introduced in that release.
