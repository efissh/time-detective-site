DeskLens Learn Privacy Policy

This policy explains how DeskLens Learn handles your learning material, local study records, microphone input, and Apple system services.

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Applies to: DeskLens Learn V1

Developer: Mona Apps

Contact: monaapps.support@gmail.com

1. The short version

DeskLens Learn does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking, a third-party analytics SDK, or a DeskLens-owned backend for its core learning workflow. Learning history and media records are stored in the app’s local storage on the user’s device.

2. What DeskLens Learn itself collects or transmits

Mona Apps does not operate a DeskLens account system or developer backend that collects your recordings, imported files, transcripts, translations, vocabulary, review state, or shadowing recordings. DeskLens Learn does not intentionally send that learning content to Mona Apps.

If you contact support, Mona Apps receives the information you choose to include in your email so that we can respond. Please do not include sensitive personal information that is not needed to describe the issue.

3. Content stored locally on your device

Depending on how you use the app, DeskLens Learn stores the following locally:

  • audio or video that you choose to import for study;
  • transcripts, translations, word timings, vocabulary, and review state;
  • recordings made for Sentence Shadowing and related learning history.

Imported media is copied into the app’s own storage, so the study copy is independent of the original file. DeskLens Learn does not provide its own account-based cloud sync or backend recovery service. Deleting the app deletes its local data, subject to normal iOS backup and restore behavior.

4. Microphone and user-selected media

Microphone access is used for Live Listening and for recording-related learning flows such as Sentence Shadowing. The app does not silently record in the background.

Audio or video selected by you may be copied into the app’s local storage and processed for transcription, translation, playback, vocabulary, review, or shadowing functions.

5. Apple Speech and Translation technologies

DeskLens Learn uses Apple Speech technologies for transcription and Apple Translation technologies for translation. These are Apple system frameworks, separate from any information that DeskLens Learn itself sends to Mona Apps.

iOS may need to download Speech recognition language resources or Translation language resources for a language you choose. The first use of a language may therefore need an internet connection. Apple manages those system-level downloads and framework operations; DeskLens Learn does not control them or operate them as a Mona Apps backend. Apple’s own terms and privacy practices apply to Apple system services.

6. What DeskLens Learn does not include

  • no account, sign-in, or registration;
  • no advertising or advertising identifier;
  • no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK;
  • no tracking or cross-app tracking;
  • no DeskLens-owned backend required for the core learning workflow;
  • no account-based cross-device sync.

7. Data deletion and policy changes

Because the app’s learning records are stored locally, removing DeskLens Learn removes its local data from the device, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior. Mona Apps does not maintain a server copy of that learning content for a separate server-deletion request.

If a future version changes how information is handled, this page will be updated and the effective date will change.
